Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

Party Hall (partyhall.io) is run by the operator named in our Terms, ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you play, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. If you have a question, write to support@partyhall.io.

The short version. You can play as a guest and give us nothing but a name for the table. If you sign in with Google, our server keeps an anonymous player id, your display name, your saved tables and your game results; your email address and photo stay in your own browser. Payments for Plus are handled by Paddle, and we never see your card details. Free players may see ads, served by our advertising partner AdinPlay; Plus players see none. We don't sell your data or use third-party analytics.

What we collect

When you play

Games run in the players' browsers, which connect to each other directly (WebRTC). To make that work:

  • The name, piece and finish you choose, and your moves and chat messages, are shown to the other players at your table.
  • Your IP address is visible to the other players at your table when your browsers connect directly, and to our matchmaking server, which introduces browsers to each other, and our relay server, which carries the connection when a direct one isn't possible. The relay passes game traffic along; it does not store it.
  • Table reports. The browser running a table sends our server a summary while it is played: the game, the table code, the players' table names and pieces, cash or score, whose turn it is, who won, the device type (phone or computer) and the language. We use these to see that the service works and to fix problems. They are kept as daily log files on our server and are never shown to other players.
  • Public tables. If a host lists a table publicly, its code, host name and seat count appear in the public list while it is open.
  • Web server logs. Like almost every website, our server records the IP address, browser type, page requested and time of each request.

When you sign in with Google

  • Google tells us your account identifier, name, email address and profile photo. We turn the identifier into an anonymous player id with a one-way keyed hash, and give your browser a signed "pass" containing that player id and your display name. The pass is shown to the hosts of the tables you join, so they know the seat is yours.
  • Your email address and photo are sent only to your own browser, to show you which account you are signed in with. They are not stored on our servers and never shown to other players.
  • Saved tables. Games you play while signed in are saved on our server so they can be resumed, including the players' table names and the state of the game. A saved table is deleted when the game ends, or 14 days after its last change (90 days if a Plus player sits at it).
  • Game history. When a game you played ends, your browser records your result on our server: the game, your place, the rounds played, and the table names and scores of the players at that table. We keep your newest 200 games.

When you buy Plus

Checkout is provided by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited), our reseller and the Merchant of Record for all orders. Paddle collects your payment details, billing address and email address and processes them under Paddle's privacy policy. We receive transaction and subscription identifiers, which plan was bought, and your anonymous player id, and we use them to switch Plus on and keep a record of your subscription. We never see or store your card details.

Stored in your browser

We use your browser's local storage for things the game needs: your table name, piece, finish and language, your sign-in pass, and a copy of a table you are hosting so a refresh doesn't lose it. You can clear these at any time in your browser's settings.

Advertising

If you don't have Plus, the front door, the lobby and the end of a game can show ads, served by AdinPlay and its advertising partners. When an ad is shown, your browser loads it from their servers, and they may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads, under AdinPlay's privacy policy. Where the law requires your consent, you are asked for it before any of this happens, and you can change your choice at any time. We do not give our advertising partners your name, email address or player id. Ads are never loaded for Plus players.

Other services our pages use

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit. Signing in uses Google's sign-in page, under Google's privacy policy. The checkout is loaded from Paddle when you choose to buy Plus.

Why we use it

To run the games you ask to play; to keep your account, seats and saved games working; to provide Plus when you pay for it; to keep the service secure and prevent abuse; and to find and fix what breaks. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on performing our agreement with you (our Terms of Service) and on our legitimate interest in running a secure, working service.

Who we share it with

  • The other players at your table, as described above.
  • Paddle, for payments, and Google, for sign-in.
  • AdinPlay and its advertising partners, for ads shown to players without Plus, as described above.
  • DigitalOcean, whose servers in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, host our service and store the data described here. A copy of the game's pages is also served by GitHub Pages.
  • Public authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Saved tables: until the game ends, or 14 days after the last change (90 days with Plus).
  • Game history: your newest 200 games, while your account is in use.
  • Plus and subscription records: while your subscription runs, and afterwards for as long as we need them for accounting, refunds and legal obligations.
  • Table reports and server logs: only as long as we need them to operate and troubleshoot the service.

We delete any of it sooner if you ask, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You can play without signing in. Plus removes all ads. You can ask us for a copy of the data linked to your player id, to correct it, or to delete it: email support@partyhall.io from the address you sign in with and include your player id (shown in the Plus panel once you are signed in). Depending on where you live, for example in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also have the right to object to or restrict our use of your data, to receive it in a portable format, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Party Hall is not directed at children under 13, and signing in requires a Google account that meets Google's age requirements. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will tell signed-in players in the game.
