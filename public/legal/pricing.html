Questions

Does everyone at my table need Plus?

No. One Plus player at a table unlocks the board themes for the whole table, and everyone sees the Plus player's piece and dice finishes.

How does billing work?

Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax or VAT may be added at checkout depending on where you live. Payments are processed securely by Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Plus renews automatically each month or year until you cancel.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, using the link in your Paddle receipt email or at paddle.net. Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Yes: within 14 days of any payment, for any reason. See our Refund Policy.
