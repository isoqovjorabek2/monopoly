Refund Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

We want Party Hall Plus to be worth it. If it isn't, you can have your money back.

14-day refunds

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any Plus payment, whether it was your first purchase or a renewal, for any reason. When a payment is refunded, the Plus time that payment bought is removed from your account.

How to ask for a refund

Payments for Plus are processed by Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record, so refunds are paid out by Paddle. You can:

  • request it at paddle.net, where you can find your order using the email address you paid with; or
  • reply to your Paddle receipt email; or
  • email us at support@partyhall.io with your order number, and we'll ask Paddle to process it.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Depending on your bank, they usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days.

Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling stops future renewals; it is not a refund. After you cancel, Plus stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you won't be charged again. You can cancel at any time using the link in your Paddle receipt email or at paddle.net.

After 14 days

Payments older than 14 days are not refundable, except where the law where you live requires a refund, or where Plus didn't work because of a problem on our side. If that happens, contact us and we'll put it right.
